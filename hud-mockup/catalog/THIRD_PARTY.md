# Third-party catalogue data

Stock catalogue identities and Russian item names derive from [@ianlucas/cs2-lib](https://github.com/ianlucas/cs2-lib), copyright Ian Lucas, distributed under the [MIT licence](third-party/cs2-lib-MIT.txt). The base repository commit and exact SHA-256 hashes of the modified source data are recorded in `provenance.json`; the modified inputs must not be represented as identical to that commit. The 4ill website composition-capabilities version, build ID and source hash are also recorded there.

Valve game artwork is referenced by `s2r://` URLs from the client-mounted game resources; the stock catalogue does not redistribute those textures. Images reused for charms identify the embedded sticker, and the graffiti tint method remains subject to client visual validation.
